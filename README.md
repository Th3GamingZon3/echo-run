# Echo Run

**Every fall leaves something behind.**

The platformer where failing is how you win: every run you end becomes a glowing crystal *echo* you
can stand on, jump from and build with. Your final run is carried by every attempt you ever made.

▶ **Play in your browser:** https://th3gamingzon3.github.io/echo-run/

- 35 hand-made levels in four worlds, with Spring Cores, crumbling floors, Dash Orbs, relay timers,
  lasers and saws
- A hidden Prism in every level, and Time Trial against your own ghost
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch

**Controls:** Arrows / A D to move · Space / W / ↑ to jump · Shift to dash · **R to leave an echo** · Esc to pause
